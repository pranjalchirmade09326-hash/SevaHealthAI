# SevaHealth AI — Public Health Pilot & Phased Rollout Plan

> **Pilot Jurisdiction**: Mysuru District, Karnataka (Nanjangud & Hunsur Taluks)  
> **Implementing Partner**: District Health Society & National Health Mission (NHM) Karnataka

---

## 1. Phased Implementation Roadmap

```
PHASE 1: Taluk Community Pilot (Months 1-3)
├── Target Population: 5,000 Citizens (Ages 30-65)
├── Frontline Staff: 25 ASHA Workers & 4 Primary Health Centres (PHCs)
├── Focus: Validating screening speed (<3m), offline sync & ASHA usability
└── Milestone: First 5,000 digital risk trajectories established; baseline cohort mapped

PHASE 2: District-Wide Scaling (Months 4-6)
├── Target Population: 100,000 Citizens across 7 Taluks in Mysuru District
├── Facilities: 42 PHCs, 6 Community Health Centres (CHCs), 1 District Hospital
├── Focus: Open Wearables deployment, Clinician Copilot queue integration
└── Milestone: 40% reduction in pre-diabetic defaulter rate; ₹37.8M projected savings verified

PHASE 3: State Health Mission Expansion (Months 7-12)
├── Target Population: 1,000,000+ Citizens across 5 High-Burden Karnataka Districts
├── Integration: Statewide Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM) Registry
├── Focus: K8s multi-zone clustering, automated geospatial hotspot allocation
└── Milestone: State-level predictive NCD early warning system operational
```

---

## 2. Frontline Training & Change Management

1. **ASHA Digital Training (3-Day Workshop)**:
   - Hands-on practice with the lightweight mobile intake portal on budget government tablets.
   - Emphasizing simple communication of the 4 vital questions to villagers.
   - Understanding automated follow-up rosters.
2. **Medical Officer Clinical Alignment (1-Day CME)**:
   - Training PHC physicians on reviewing pre-compiled SOAP triage cases in the Clinician Copilot.
   - Setting clear guidelines for approving vs overriding AI-suggested lifestyle care plans.

---

## 3. Evaluation Milestones & KPIs

| Phase | Core Milestone KPI | Target Metric | Verification Method |
|:---|:---|:---:|:---|
| **Phase 1** | Community Screening Throughput | > 20 citizens/ASHA/week | Automated system timestamp audit |
| **Phase 1** | Offline Data Conflict Rate | < 0.2% collision rate | Conflict resolution sync logs |
| **Phase 2** | Pre-diabetes Lifestyle Adherence | > 65% habit completion | Citizen mobile app check-ins + wearable telemetry |
| **Phase 2** | Clinician Escalation Turnaround | < 24 hours from alert | Clinician digital stamp timestamp |
| **Phase 3** | Re-screening Follow-up Rate | > 80% at 90 days | Population intelligence defaulter ledger |
