## 3. Empowerment of the Frontline Health Workforce

India's 1,000,000+ ASHA (Accredited Social Health Activist) and ANM workers are the backbone of the nation's health delivery. However, they are overwhelmed by bureaucratic manual reporting.

SevaHealth AI transforms the health worker's daily reality:
1. **Zero Math**: No manual calculation of IDRS scores or BMI tables.
2. **Offline Resilience**: Works without cellular signal in remote villages; auto-syncs when returning to the PHC.
3. **Actionable Task Rosters**: Replaces stacks of paper registers with an automated morning task list:
   - *"Visit Ramesh Patel: Scheduled 30-day BP follow-up due today."*
   - *"Escort Lakshmi Devi: Critical blood pressure detected; PHC doctor review arranged."*
4. **Vernacular Audio Prompts**: Health workers can play translated audio explanations directly to illiterate citizens, demystifying their health data.
