# SevaHealth AI: Clinical Safety & AI Governance Framework

**Document Version:** 1.0.0  
**Target:** SevaHealth AI Platform  
**Clinical Standards:** WHO Guidelines on AI in Health, ICMR Guidelines for AI in Healthcare (2023), US FDA Action Plan for AI/ML-Based Software as a Medical Device (SaMD)

---

## 1. Foundational Clinical Safety Philosophy

SevaHealth AI is a **preventive-health decision-intelligence platform**, NOT an autonomous diagnostic or prescribing system.

The core principle governing every AI model, algorithmic heuristic, and agent workflow in SevaHealth AI is:

> **"AI empowers and stratifies; licensed human clinicians diagnose and prescribe."**

The platform maintains an unbreakable boundary between **probabilistic preventive risk estimation** and **definitive clinical diagnosis**. The system must NEVER present a probabilistic calculation as a confirmed disease, nor make unilateral clinical decisions.

---

## 2. Permitted vs. Strictly Prohibited AI Capabilities

```
+----------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+
|               PERMITTED AI CAPABILITIES            |          STRICTLY PROHIBITED CAPABILITIES          |
+----------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+
| 1. Summarize screening questionnaire responses     | 1. Independently issue a clinical diagnosis        |
| 2. Stratify preventive multi-factor NCD risk       | 2. Prescribe any pharmaceutical medication         |
| 3. Identify longitudinal physiological patterns    | 3. Alter, titrate, or discontinue existing meds    |
| 4. Explain underlying risk drivers (SHAP-style)    | 4. Issue formal lab, imaging, or medical orders    |
| 5. Recommend preventive lifestyle interventions    | 5. Claim diagnostic certainty when evidence is low |
| 6. Flag critical thresholds for clinical review    | 6. Supersede or bypass human physician judgment    |
| 7. Generate draft SOAP pre-consultation notes      | 7. Perform autonomous emergency triage dispatch    |
+----------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+
```

## 3. Strict Categorization of Clinical Functions

To avoid confusion among citizens and healthcare workers, the system strictly separates its functional categories:

```
[SCREENING]                 → Collection of demographic, lifestyle, and survey data (CBAC/IDRS).
      ↓
[RISK ESTIMATION]           → Algorithmic calculation of statistical probability (Low, Mod, High, Critical).
      ↓
[DECISION SUPPORT (AI)]     → Identifying top contributing drivers, drafting 30-day habits, synthesizing summaries.
      ↓
============================ CRITICAL HUMAN-IN-THE-LOOP BOUNDARY ============================
      ↓
[DIAGNOSIS (HUMAN ONLY)]    → Formal identification of disease state by a registered medical practitioner.
      ↓
[TREATMENT (HUMAN ONLY)]    → Prescription of pharmacological therapy, clinical orders, or medical procedures.
      ↓
[EMERGENCY ESCALATION]      → Immediate urgent protocol routing when vitals exceed safety thresholds.
```

## 4. Mandatory Clinical Safety Notice & Disclaimers

### 4.1. Universal Disclaimer
Every screen, report, API response, and export containing AI-generated risk scores or care plans must prominently display:

> **"CLINICAL REVIEW RECOMMENDED: This assessment is an AI-assisted preventive health screening tool based on population guidelines. It does NOT constitute a medical diagnosis or treatment plan. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical evaluation."**

### 4.2. High-Risk / Critical Escalation Notice
Whenever an overall risk score enters `HIGH` or `CRITICAL` tiers (or biometric vitals exceed critical thresholds: Systolic BP > 160 mmHg, Fasting Glucose > 200 mg/dL):

* The UI automatically emphasizes in amber/red badges: **"URGENT CLINICAL REVIEW REQUIRED"**.
* The citizen is given a clear button: **"Connect with Medical Officer / Primary Health Center"**.
* The case is automatically enqueued into the **Clinician Triage Inbox**.

## 5. Metadata Architecture for AI-Generated Outputs

Every recommendation, risk breakdown, and care plan generated by the AI runtime must carry mandatory clinical provenance metadata:

```python
class ClinicalSafetyMetadata(BaseModel):
    confidence_score: float           # 0.0 to 1.0 (statistical confidence based on inputs)
    uncertainty_level: str            # "LOW", "MODERATE", "HIGH" (based on missing labs/vitals)
    evidence_basis: List[str]         # e.g., ["ICMR-INDIAB Guidelines 2023", "WHO PEN Protocol"]
    source_data_snapshot: Dict[str, Any] # Hash or copy of vitals/labs evaluated
    model_provider: str               # e.g., "Google-Gemini-1.5-Pro" or "Deterministic-Rules-Engine"
    model_version: str                # e.g., "v1.2.0-clinical"
    generated_at: datetime
    human_review_state: HumanReviewState # PENDING_REVIEW, CLINICIAN_APPROVED, MODIFIED
    reviewing_clinician_id: Optional[str]
    reviewed_at: Optional[datetime]
```

If vital lab inputs are missing (e.g., citizen provided only waist circumference and age, but no blood pressure or glucose), the system:
1. Lowers the `confidence_score`.
2. Marks `uncertainty_level` as `HIGH`.
3. Explicitly prompts the citizen: *"Lab tests (HbA1c / Lipid profile) are required to complete this assessment with full confidence."*

## 6. Hybrid Risk Evaluation: Deterministic Guidelines + AI Structuring

To eliminate hallucinations and guarantee mathematical repeatability, SevaHealth AI uses a **two-tier evaluation engine**:

```
[Raw Citizen Vitals & Screening Data]
                  │
                  ▼
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  Tier 1: Deterministic Clinical Guidelines Engine      │
│  - Mathematical ICMR Indian Diabetes Risk Score (IDRS) │
│  - ACC/AHA 2017 Blood Pressure Staging Formulas        │
│  - WHO/ISH 10-Year Cardiovascular Risk Matrix          │
│  - Exact Cutoff Classifications (No LLM hallucinations)│
└─────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────┘
                          │
                          ▼ (Verified Baseline Scores)
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  Tier 2: AI Decision Intelligence & Explainability     │
│  - Synthesizes multi-factor relationships (SHAP-style) │
│  - Generates empathetic, culturally tailored lifestyle │
│    interventions (millets, local walking routines)     │
│  - Formulates structured clinician pre-visit summaries  │
│  - Enforces strict Pydantic JSON schema output         │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

If an external LLM API is unavailable, the Tier 1 deterministic engine functions autonomously with a local rule-based explanation template, guaranteeing zero downtime and complete offline testability.

## 7. Human-in-the-Loop Review State Machine

Clinical triage cases and care plans follow a formal state machine:

```
[DRAFT / AI_GENERATED]
         │
         ├─── (Clinician reviews without edits) ────► [CLINICIAN_APPROVED]
         │
         ├─── (Clinician adjusts targets/tasks) ───► [CLINICIAN_MODIFIED]
         │
         └─── (Clinician flags immediate danger) ──► [ESCALATED_TO_SPECIALIST]
```

No AI-generated recommendation is marked as verified until a licensed practitioner advances the state.
