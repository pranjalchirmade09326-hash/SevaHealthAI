# SevaHealth AI: Community Health Worker (ASHA / ANM) Field Terminal

**Platform:** Responsive Mobile Web / Tablet Portal  
**Primary Users:** Accredited Social Health Activists (ASHA), Auxiliary Nurse Midwives (ANM), Community Health Officers (CHO)  
**Standard Alignments:** National Health Mission (NHM) NCD Screening Guidelines, Ayushman Bharat Comprehensive Primary Health Care  

---

## Field Screening Features

1. **Rapid Citizen Registration:**
   - Captures demographic details, phone number, and ABHA ID.
2. **Community Based Assessment Checklist (CBAC):**
   - Standard 6-part risk questionnaire for early detection of diabetes, hypertension, and oral/breast/cervical cancers.
3. **Indian Diabetes Risk Score (IDRS):**
   - Rapid score out of 100 points based on age, waist circumference, physical activity, and family history.
4. **Vitals & Point-of-Care Lab Capture:**
   - Digital BP measurement, glucometer fasting glucose, waist tape measure.
5. **Instant Field Referral Flag:**
   - Automatically alerts the health worker when vitals exceed safety cutoffs (e.g., BP >= 140/90 or FBG >= 126 mg/dL) to issue a formal Primary Health Centre referral slip.
