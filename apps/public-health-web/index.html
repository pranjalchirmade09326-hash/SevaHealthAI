5. WHAT SHOULD THE PROGRAM PRIORITIZE?

Top 5 AI Resource Allocation Directives & Public Health Intervention Prioritizations.

PRIORITY 1 • HIGHEST IMPACT Surveillance Hotspot Interception

Deploy 2 Rapid Mobile Screening Vans to Devanahalli Ward 12 & Mandya Central

Rationale: 34.2% of screened citizens in Ward 12 exhibit undetected high metabolic risk. Expanding mobile reach will intercept an estimated 2,500 unreached citizens before acute cardiovascular complications develop.

Projected Outcome: +18% screening coverage in industrial corridor; 280+ early prediabetes cases intercepted.
PRIORITY 2 • TARGETED COHORT Asymptomatic Prevention

Launch Workplace Metabolic Screening Initiative for Age Group 30–44

Rationale: 32.4% of citizens in this productive working-age cohort show silent prediabetes and early dyslipidemia with zero clinical symptoms. Work-site screening removes scheduling barriers.

Projected Outcome: 4,000 workplace evaluations across 12 tech and manufacturing facilities; 85% enrollment in habit coaching.
PRIORITY 3 • SUPPLY CHAIN Diagnostic Infrastructure

Emergency Consumables Restock: Glucometer Test Strips & Auto-BP Cuffs at 18 PHCs

Rationale: 64% of follow-up delays in Mandya and Chamarajanagar were caused by stock-outs of point-of-care test strips. Directing replenishment to 18 high-volume primary centres.

Projected Outcome: Diagnostic turnaround drops from 8.4 days to < 2 days; referral drop-off cut by 45%.
PRIORITY 4 • CLINICAL SAFETY High-Risk Patient Retention

Intensive ASHA Home-Visit Campaign for 284 Overdue High-Risk Citizens

Rationale: Hypertensive and diabetic citizens overdue > 30 days face high risk of acute vascular events and hospitalizations. Deploying dedicated ASHA stipends for home vitals verification.

Projected Outcome: 92% re-engagement within 14 days; zero preventable emergency hospitalizations.
PRIORITY 5 • POPULATION NUTRITION Primary Community Prevention

State-Wide Dietary Sodium & Refined Carbohydrate Public Education Drive

Rationale: 68% of surveyed households report daily sodium intake exceeding 8.5g (WHO rec: < 5g). Disseminating vernacular audio messages via SevaHealth citizen WhatsApp bridge and community radio.

Projected Outcome: Estimated 4–6% community-level reduction in mean population sodium intake over 6 months.
