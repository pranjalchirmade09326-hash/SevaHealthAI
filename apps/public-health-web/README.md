# SevaHealth AI: Population Health & Epidemiological Intelligence Dashboard

**Platform:** Web Portal (Next.js / Chart.js / Tailwind CSS)  
**Primary Users:** District Health Officers (DHO), State Health Secretaries, Public Health Epidemiologists  

---

## Core Epidemiological Capabilities

1. **Prevalence Heatmaps:**
   - Visualizes pre-diabetes, pre-hypertension, and metabolic syndrome clusters across districts and primary health centres.
2. **Risk Tier Stratification:**
   - Population breakdown into Low, Moderate, High, and Critical risk cohorts.
3. **Intervention Outcome Efficacy Delta:**
   - Longitudinal measurement comparing citizens with high adherence (>=75%) to preventive care plans versus low adherence (<40%).
   - Demonstrates measurable clinical outcome improvements (average -6.4 mmHg systolic BP drop and -14.2 mg/dL fasting glucose reduction).
4. **Healthcare Resource Allocation:**
   - Identifies high-risk demographic clusters requiring mobile health camp deployments or specialized diabetes education drives.
